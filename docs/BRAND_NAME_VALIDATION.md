## Decision

# Does It Automate?

Handle: **@DoesItAutomate**

The channel owner has claimed and is operating the YouTube identity `DoesItAutomate / @DoesItAutomate`.

The earlier indexed-collision screen did not identify an established same-category public identity dominating the exact phrase at the time of validation.

This is an operational brand freeze, not a legal trademark opinion.

- every episode can answer it with evidence,
- supports builds, experiments, comparisons and business economics,
- not tied to one AI vendor,
- works with aggressive evidence-backed packaging,
- naturally reinforces the channel thesis.
