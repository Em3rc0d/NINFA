# MK0 Status

## Decision

**GO**

Project NINFA has sufficient initial evidence to proceed from broad market validation into channel identity, first-batch planning, and production prototyping.

## Frozen decisions

- Language: English.
- Primary distribution: YouTube long-form.
- Core niche: practical AI systems, software, agents, and automation.
- Primary value proposition: real builds, real tests, real outcomes.
- Primary audience: technical professionals, builders, solopreneurs, freelancers, agencies, and automation-curious business users.
- Geographic monetization priority: Tier-1 English-speaking markets.
- Long-form operating range: 8–16 minutes; normally 10–14 when justified.
- Packaging: aggressive curiosity/clickbait-style framing is allowed when the video fulfills the promise.
- Automation target: 90–95% of production operations.
- Human role: editor-in-chief / approval gate, not manual video operator.
- Initial cadence target after stabilization: ~4 long-form videos/week.
- Monetization: affiliates from video #1 where genuinely relevant; later ads, sponsors, products and owned software.
- Budget: flexible; optimize return and learning per dollar rather than spending a fixed allowance.

## Market patterns observed

Initial research supports several recurring high-interest patterns in the AI/software/automation niche:

- build-and-sell,
- first useful AI agent,
- complete practical courses,
- replace an existing tool/workflow,
- tool-vs-tool decisions,
- real automation experiments,
- business outcomes from AI,
- cost/ROI transparency.

The working conclusion is that **outcome-driven content is a stronger foundation than generic AI news or generic top-tools lists**.

## MK0 risks

1. AI-slop perception if production becomes templated or visually generic.
2. Weak retention if videos are padded to reach duration targets.
3. High AI-video cost if motion generation is overused.
4. Overengineering the engine before publishing enough real videos.
5. Depending on affiliate economics before traffic exists.
6. Chasing trending AI news at the expense of evergreen utility.
7. Reused open-source code becoming a maintenance/security liability without audit.

## Next gate — MK0.1

MK0.1 passes when:

- channel identity is frozen,
- first six long-form topics are evidence-backed and locked,
- thumbnail/title hypothesis exists for each,
- production stack is selected,
- first end-to-end private/unlisted prototype is rendered,
- actual production cost/time is measured,
- QA standard is defined,
- video #1 is publication-ready.
