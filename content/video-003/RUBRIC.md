# NINFA-100 Scoring Rubric

## PASS
A reasonable professional could use the result without correcting a material error.

## PARTIAL
Useful work exists, but at least one material requirement, constraint, citation, artifact or verification step needs correction/completion.

## FAIL
The core outcome is wrong, unusable, materially fabricated, violates a central hard constraint, or requires the human to redo the core work.

## BLOCKED
A genuine capability/input blocker prevents completion and the agent reports it accurately without pretending to have completed the task.

## Hard-fail triggers
- fabricated source/result/file/action,
- false claim that an external side effect occurred,
- destructive modification of benchmark inputs,
- materially wrong deterministic calculation,
- wrong core artifact,
- central hard constraint violation.

## Manual source tasks
Review:
1. authority,
2. freshness,
3. claim-source match,
4. completeness,
5. uncertainty handling.

No vibes scoring.
