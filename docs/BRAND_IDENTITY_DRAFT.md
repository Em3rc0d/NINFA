# Brand Identity — Draft v0.1

> Status: **DRAFT — NOT FROZEN**

## Brand architecture

- **Project NINFA** remains the internal project / autonomous media engine name.
- The public YouTube channel should use a distinct commercial brand.

## Current frontrunner

# Actually Automated

**Working promise:** AI systems. Real work. Real results.

**Positioning:** an independent technology lab/media brand that builds, tests, and explains AI systems and automations using real workflows, measurable outcomes, and honest failure data.

## Why this name currently leads

- It is memorable and naturally tied to the channel's anti-hype / proof-driven thesis.
- It supports aggressive YouTube packaging without requiring a presenter-led personal brand.
- It creates an editorial question that can recur across videos: is this *actually automated*?
- Preliminary web collision checks did not identify an established AI/automation channel or major product dominating the exact name.
- More generic territories such as “AI That Works” and “Systems That Work” already collide with active companies/content and are therefore weaker brand candidates.

## Brand promise

> We build, test, and explain AI systems that actually do useful work.

Extended formulation:

> We test AI agents, automation systems and emerging software by building real things with them, measuring the results and showing what is actually worth using.

## Editorial personality

- Curious
- Technical but accessible
- Skeptical of hype
- Outcome-driven
- Experimental
- Entertaining without pretending
- Comfortable showing failures
- Commercially aware without becoming affiliate-first

The voice should feel like an engineer willing to try an ambitious claim and disciplined enough to measure what actually happened.

## Packaging doctrine

**Maximum curiosity without breaking the promise.**

Aggressive titles and thumbnails are encouraged when supported by the actual video.

Examples:
- I Built an AI Employee for $17
- I Gave an AI Agent 100 Real Tasks
- I Automated a YouTube Channel for 30 Days
- n8n Might Have a Serious Problem
- I Replaced 3 SaaS Tools With One AI Agent

## Visual direction

Avoid generic AI-brand aesthetics such as purple gradients, glowing robot heads, circuit brains, and interchangeable synthetic imagery.

Preferred system:
- graphite / near-black base,
- off-white typography,
- one high-energy signal accent,
- real software screenshots,
- numbers and measured outcomes,
- arrows / annotations / failure states,
- technical diagram language,
- minimal synthetic decoration.

Target feeling: **engineering lab + experiment dashboard + YouTube entertainment**.

## Thumbnail language

Potential recurring high-salience phrases:

- FAILED 37%
- $17 / MONTH
- 24 HOURS
- 7 AGENTS
- 3 TOOLS → 1
- NOT READY
- IT WORKED
- $0 → $___

Thumbnails should prioritize real evidence, software UI, experiment outputs, comparisons, and readable stakes.

## Avatar / logo direction

Do not use a robot mascot.

Explore a compact mark around:
- AA monogram,
- execution/play symbol,
- completion/check state,
- automation loop.

It must remain recognizable at small YouTube avatar size.

## Banner working copy

**ACTUALLY AUTOMATED**

AI systems. Real work. Real results.

BUILD · TEST · AUTOMATE

## Alternative naming territories

1. **AI Under Load** — strong engineering/stress-test identity; more technical and narrower.
2. **Real AI Work** — very clear promise but less distinctive and has adjacent naming collisions.

## Before freeze

The public name may be frozen only after checking:
- YouTube channel/name collisions,
- YouTube handle availability,
- major social handle collisions,
- reasonable domain options,
- obvious trademark / same-category conflicts,
- pronunciation and recall,
- thumbnail/banner visual fit.
