# Brand Identity — v1.0

Status: **FROZEN FOR YOUTUBE LAUNCH**

## Public brand

**Does It Automate?**

Handle: **@DoesItAutomate**

Internal project: **Project NINFA — Autonomous AI Media Engine**

## Promise

**We test AI systems on real work.**

## Positioning

An English-language technology media brand that builds, tests, and explains AI agents, automation systems and software through real experiments, measurable outcomes and transparent failures.

## Logo system

Primary symbol: automation/execution mark combining:
- workflow input,
- execution,
- verified outcome,
- a strong D-shaped silhouette tied to the brand name.

Primary YouTube avatar uses a dark high-contrast background.

The symbol should be usable without text.

## Editorial personality

Curious, technical, accessible, skeptical of hype, experimental, commercially aware, outcome-driven.

## Packaging rule

**Maximum curiosity without breaking the promise.**

Aggressive titles and thumbnails are encouraged when the video actually delivers the claimed experiment/outcome.

## Visual language

Engineering lab + experiment dashboard + YouTube entertainment.

Avoid generic AI clichés.
