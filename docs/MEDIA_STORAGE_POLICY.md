# Media Storage Policy

Status: **ACTIVE**

## Goal

Keep NINFA repositories lightweight and reproducible without duplicating large video binaries.

## GitHub

Store:
- Markdown documentation,
- source code,
- scene manifests,
- scripts,
- captions,
- thumbnail briefs,
- small reference images when essential,
- analytics schemas,
- publication metadata.

Do not store:
- final long-form MP4 renders,
- large raw captures,
- large intermediate renders,
- redundant audio/video caches.

## Google Drive

Store:
- canonical brand guides/assets,
- lightweight scripts,
- SRT/caption files,
- thumbnails/carousels,
- publication notes,
- research exports,
- analytics snapshots.

Project-owner rule:
> **Do not upload the heavy final videos.**

## YouTube

YouTube is the delivery surface for final published long-form video.

## Reproducibility minimum

For every episode preserve:
- final script,
- scene manifest,
- narrator/voice configuration,
- evidence/source list,
- thumbnail/title decision,
- SRT,
- render configuration/version,
- publication URL/status when known.
